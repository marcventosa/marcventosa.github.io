*Vestuari a la platja de la Barceloneta*
Taller Carme Ribas & Miquel Mariner, $el tercer temps$
/
The project invites you to find a hidden shell in the fullness. A contrivance. The void that a body creates when it wrinkles is filled with a paste that dries with a clear gravitational impulse. These poche pieces are strategically grouped; they will become the void of a shell, an enveloping covering. Two incisions cut into this thin film, revealing an entry and exit filter for what will become a new beach wardrobe. A ritual of the naked, of getting dressed, and of finally undressing completely. The change in scale allows a generative body to close a space where human bodies hide, breathe, dry off, enter, and exit. The unit of measurement remains what is between two hands or two feet.
