*Vestuari a la platja de la Barceloneta*
Taller Carme Ribas & Miquel Mariner, $el tercer temps$
/
The project invites you to find a shell hidden inside the plenum. The cavities that are formed when the body is wrinkled are filled with modelling paste, which when dried fixes its footprints under the impact of gravity. The resulting pieces are grouped and wrapped with papier-mâché, building a thin shell that transforms the whole into a void. Two incisions pierce it and make this interior accessible:an entrance and an exit that turn the shell into a beach wardrobe. Going in naked, hiding, breathing, wiping, dressing, and going out. A body that, changing scale, becomes architecture and welcomes other bodies. The measurement continues to be that of the body: the space between two hands, between two feet.
