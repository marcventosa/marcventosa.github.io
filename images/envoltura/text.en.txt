*Vestuari a la platja de la Barceloneta*
Taller Carme Ribas & Miquel Mariner, $el tercer temps$
/
The project invites you to find a hidden shell in the plenum. An artifice . The void that a body generates when it wrinkles is filled with a paste that dries with a clear gravitational impact. These pieces, as soon as they are strategically grouped, will become the hollow of a shell, a wrapper that wraps them up. Two incisions cut out this thin film, which gives a glimpse of an entrance and exit filter in what will be a new costume for the beach.A ritual of what is naked, dressed, and finally completely undressed. The change of scale allows a generating body to close a space where human bodies hide, breathe, wipe, enter and exit. The unit of measurement remains what lies between two hands or two feet.
