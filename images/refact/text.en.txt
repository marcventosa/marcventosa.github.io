*Boavista, Porto*
Taller Belén Ramos

Office complex workshop in Porto. 
 Formerly, a factory complex was the centerpiece of a terrain between fields and some small urban settlements. For decades, this area has been gradually built up with varied uses, occupying green spaces that rain had watered and hiding streams that were once part of the urban landscape's soundscape. 

The topography shapes the site like a natural open aquifer. The complex is positioned to be protected from potential flooding; the new channel serves both as a pathway and a barrier. Water collected by the roofs system is channeled through downspouts back to the channel. In this way, the water cycle is closed and the new construction's footprint is minimized.
