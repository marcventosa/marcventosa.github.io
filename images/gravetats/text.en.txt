*Es Molinar, Palma de Mallorca*
Taller Arnau Sastre, $hcc etsab$
/
A repetition of steel trusses crosses the entire plot and becomes a white canvas that the project takes as a starting point. On this support, the space is not filled homogeneously, but is stressed in gravities: elements that attract uses and looks. 

These devices appear in contact with the border built in the interior of the island, increasing the potential of the oldcourtyards of stone, and also within the new dwellings, exploring new ways of programming and deprogramming such stays. The courtyards of floor plan Baja act as filters of ventilation and intimacy, while the modules that open towards the interior of the island transform this level into a space for meeting, exhibition and coexistence, where daily life is amplified as a collective event.
