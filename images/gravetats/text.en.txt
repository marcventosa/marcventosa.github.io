*Es Molinar, Palma de Mallorca*
Taller Arnau Sastre, $hcc etsab$
/
A repetition of overlaps crosses the entire plot and becomes a white canvas that the project takes as a starting point. On this support, the space is not filled homogeneously, but is stressed in gravities: elements that attract uses and looks.

These devices appear in contact with the built border of the interior of the island, increasing the potential of the old stone courtyards, and also within the new interiors,exploring new ways to schedule and unschedule such stays. The ground floor courtyards act as filters of ventilation and intimacy, while the modules that open towards the interior of the island transform this level into a space for meeting, exhibition and coexistence, where daily life is amplified as a collective event.
