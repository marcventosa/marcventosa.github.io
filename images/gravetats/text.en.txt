*Es Molinar, Palma de Mallorca*
Taller Arnau Sastre, $hcc etsab$
/
A repetition of steel trusses traverses all of plot and becomes a blank canvas that the project takes as its starting point. On this support, the space is not filled homogeneously but is instead tensioned by gravities: elements that attract uses and gazes. 

These devices appear in contact with the built boundary of the inner block, enhancing the potential of the old stone courtyardss, and also within the new dwellingss, exploring new ways to program and unprogram such spaces. The courtyardss of the lower floor plan act as filters for ventilation and privacy, while the moduless that open onto the courtyard transform this level into a space for gathering, exhibition, and socializing, where everyday life is amplified as a collective experience.
