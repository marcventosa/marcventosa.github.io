*refugi, clima i barri*
/
Montjuïc is more than an iconic mountain within Barcelona's urban fabric; it is the summit that has witnessed its historical evolution. In this context, this intervention is located between two significant ascents of the mountain.

The emergence of the 'mountain refuge' is proposed, activating a space that fosters contemplation and gathering in a place steeped in history, like Casaramona or Mies's pavilion, and monumental in scale, yet with a gaze toward the neighborhood and its people. The project also includes the transformation of the 8 Hall facade, making it more permeable and inviting interaction, while reinforcing the expression of the architectural proposal.
