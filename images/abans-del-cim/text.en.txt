*refugi, clima i barri*
/
Montjuïc is more than an emblematic mountain within the urban fabric of Barcelona; it is the summit that has witnessed its historical evolution. In this context, this intervention is located between two significant mountain climbs.

The appearance of the ‘mountain refuge’ is proposed, activating a space that favors contemplation and encounter, in a place full of history, such as Casaramona or the pavilion of Mies, and monumentalitybut with a look at the neighborhood and its people. The project also includes the transformation of the façade of Hall 8, making it more permeable and inviting interaction, while reinforcing the expression of the architectural proposal.
