En Pau té 24 anys, és el germà del mig i treballa com a comunicador i dissenyador en una empresa alternativa. Li encanta la seva feina, però encara més arribar a casa al final del dia i mirar com la llum de la tarda entra per la finestra, recordant-li Menorca. Té debilitat per l’estiu, les tardes llargues i l’arquitectura mediterrània.
However, what really drives him is dance. He has been dancing since he was a child, and for him it is as much a refuge as a way to connect with space. When he dances, everything falls into place: the body, the air, the silence. It is another way of communicating, more sincere than any word.

Pau is six feet tall, but he doesn't define himself by it. He's only grateful for it when he can move freely, without bumping into ceilings or tripping over chairs. In the end, it all comes down to this: finding spaces where he can be himself.
