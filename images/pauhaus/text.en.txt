En Pau té 24 anys, és el germà del mig i treballa com a comunicador i dissenyador en una empresa alternativa. Li encanta la seva feina, però encara més arribar a casa al final del dia i mirar com la llum de la tarda entra per la finestra, recordant-li Menorca. Té debilitat per l’estiu, les tardes llargues i l’arquitectura mediterrània.
However, what really moves him is the dance. Dancing from an early age, it's both a haven and a way to connect with the space. When she dances, everything fits: the body, the air, the silence. It is another way of communicating, more sincere than any word.

Pau is two meters high, but it is not defined. He only appreciates it when he can move freely, without running into ceilings or crossing himself with chairs. In the end, it all comes down to this: finding spaces to be himself.
